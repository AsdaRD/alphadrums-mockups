FAQ — specs · activation

Answers, straight.

Does it run in my DAW?+

AlphaDrums ships as VST3 and AU plus a standalone app, for macOS and Windows. If your DAW hosts VST3 or AU, you're covered.

How does offline activation work?+

Your license key is personalised and cryptographically signed for you. The plugin verifies it locally — no activation server, no phone-home, ever.

How heavy is it on disk and RAM?+

One ~512 MB sample bank, loaded lazily in the background. Only the active snare shell stays in RAM — and the meter shows the real number.

Will it fit my MIDI mapping?+

Six factory maps (GM, GGD, SD3, SSD5, EZD and native) plus MIDI Learn — click a drum, hit a pad, done.
